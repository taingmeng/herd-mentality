## 🎲 Section 1: Board Games, Toys & Geek Culture (401-440)

**401. How many side faces are there on a standard, traditional D&D die used to calculate percentage outcomes (the percentile d10)?**

* **Answer:** 10
* **Fun Fact:** While it features 10 triangular faces, players usually roll a pair of these together—one tracking tens (10, 20, 30) and one tracking single digits—to generate a clean 1-to-100 random score.

**402. In what year did the classic cooperative board game *Pandemic* launch, tasking players with halting global outbreaks?**

* **Answer:** 2008
* **Fun Fact:** Designed by Matt Leacock, it completely altered modern board game history by popularizing strict team-versus-the-board cooperative mechanics.

**403. How many total resource hex tiles make up the island map grid layout in a base game of *Catan*?**

* **Answer:** 19
* **Fun Fact:** This includes 4 forests, 4 pastures, 4 fields, 3 hills, 3 mountains, and exactly 1 central desert tile that yields zero resources.

**404. What is the standard number of cards dealt to each player at the start of a game of *Magic: The Gathering*?**

* **Answer:** 7
* **Fun Fact:** If a player isn't happy with their starting hand, they can choose to take a "mulligan," shuffling their deck to draw a fresh hand with varying rules depending on the format.

**405. In what year was the classic tile-placement game *Carcassonne* released, introducing the board gaming world to "meeples"?**

* **Answer:** 2000
* **Fun Fact:** The little wooden person tokens were dubbed "meeples" by a player during a game session as a portmanteau of "my people," a term that became industry shorthand.

**406. How many total property spaces are available for direct purchase on a standard, classic Monopoly board?**

* **Answer:** 22
* **Fun Fact:** This count focuses strictly on the color-coded neighborhoods (from Mediterranean Avenue to Boardwalk), completely excluding the 4 railroads and 2 utilities.

* **Answer:** 10
* **Fun Fact:** "Z" and "Q" share the title of the highest-scoring single letter tiles in the game, with only a single tile of each letter included in a standard 100-tile pouch.

**408. In what year did the classic strategic deduction game *The Resistance* drop, tasking players with rooting out hidden spies?**

* **Answer:** 2009

**410. What is the minimum number of deck cards legally required to build a valid constructed tournament deck in *Magic: The Gathering*?**

**411. In what year was the massive social deduction party game *Codenames* published by Vlaada Chvátil?**

## 🎮 Section 2: Video Game Lore & Industry History (441-480)

**441. In what year did Nintendo release *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* on the N64, redefining 3D gaming?**

* **Answer:** 1998
* **Fun Fact:** It became the first video game to secure a perfect 40/40 score from Japan's elite *Famitsu* magazine, popularizing the concept of contextual 3D target-locking (Z-targeting).

**442. How many bits of processing architecture powered the iconic Atari 2600 home video game console?**

* **Answer:** 8-bit
* **Fun Fact:** Powered by a MOS Technology 6507 chip running at a modest 1.19 MHz, the system had a mere 128 bytes of RAM built directly into its console architecture.

**443. What is the top flying speed, in miles per hour, of the iconic F-Zero racing machine the "Blue Falcon" driven by Captain Falcon?**

* **Answer:** 570 mph
* **Fun Fact:** The anti-gravity craft utilizes twin custom plasma engines, designed to push past standard speeds on sci-fi tracks elevated high over cityscapes.

**444. How many distinct structural levels make up the original arcade version of *Donkey Kong* released in 1981?**

* **Answer:** 4
* **Fun Fact:** The classic stages are the Girders (25m), Conveyors (50m), Elevators (75m), and Rivets (100m). Jumpman (Mario) had to pull the rivets out to send Donkey Kong crashing down.

**445. In what year did Valve officially launch the digital PC gaming platform Steam, radically reshaping game distribution?**

* **Answer:** 2003
* **Fun Fact:** Steam was originally met with intense pushback from PC gamers, as it launched primarily as a mandatory tool to push automated updates for *Counter-Strike 1.6*.

**446. How many distinct basic Pokemon species made up the original Pokedex generation in *Pokemon Red & Blue*?**

* **Answer:** 151
* **Fun Fact:** The 151st Pokemon, Mew, was secretly coded into the game by programmer Shigeki Morimoto at the last second without Nintendo's knowledge, spark-plugging massive schoolyard myths.

**447. What is the standard number of inventory slot squares available to a player in a base starting pack in *Minecraft*?**

* **Answer:** 36 slots
* **Fun Fact:** This includes 9 hotbar slots across the bottom of the screen and a 27-slot storage matrix hidden inside the player inventory menu interface.

**448. How many characters long is the maximum text length allowed for a custom seed code in *Minecraft* Java Edition?**

* **Answer:** 20 characters
* **Fun Fact:** If you type out custom words instead of numbers, the game runs a hashing algorithm to convert the letters into a 64-bit numeric string to generate the terrain.

**449. In what year did the massive multi-player online RPG *World of Warcraft* debut, setting the standard for the genre?**

* **Answer:** 2004
* **Fun Fact:** The launch was so massively crowded that Blizzard developers had to halt physical shipments of the game to retail stores to keep login servers from melting under the load.

**450. How many individual frames of animation make up the iconic character sprite for Sonic the Hedgehog in his original 1991 Sega Genesis title?**

* **Answer:** 40
* **Fun Fact:** This tight pool handled everything from his sprint and spin-dash to his famous, impatient foot-tapping idle animation when the player left the controller alone.

**451. What is the maximum height, in block units, that a player can build up to in a modern *Minecraft* world map?**

## 🏛️ Section 3: Mythology, Folklore & Ancient Beliefs (481-520)

**481. In Norse mythology, how many distinct realms are connected together by the roots and branches of the world tree, Yggdrasil?**

* **Answer:** 9
* **Fun Fact:** These realms include Asgard (home of the Aesir gods), Midgard (human world), Helheim (realm of the dead), and Muspelheim (realm of fire).

**482. How many Labors was the Greek hero Heracles (Hercules) traditionally forced to complete to atone for his crimes?**

* **Answer:** 12
* **Fun Fact:** He was originally tasked with only 10 labors, but King Eurystheus disqualified two tasks (slaying the Hydra and cleaning the Augean stables) because Heracles accepted help and payment.

**483. What is the total height of the famous Great Sphinx of Giza, carved out of a single ridge of limestone, in feet?**

* **Answer:** 66 feet
* **Fun Fact:** The monument combines the body of a crouching lion with a human head, stretching 240 feet long from its front paws to its tail.

**484. How many main principal deities form the core of the ancient Egyptian sun god Ra's supreme court (the Ennead of Heliopolis)?**

* **Answer:** 9
* **Fun Fact:** The group consists of Atum and his direct lineage: Shu, Tefnut, Geb, Nut, Osiris, Isis, Set, and Nephthys.

**485. In what year BC did the Greek philosopher Plato first write about the legendary sunken continent of Atlantis in his dialogues?**

* **Answer:** 360 BC
* **Fun Fact:** Plato introduced Atlantis in his works *Timaeus* and *Critias* purely as a cautionary philosophical fable about the dangers of imperial hubris and greed.

**486. How many days long did the ancient Babylonian festival of Akitu last to celebrate the rebirth of nature?**

* **Answer:** 12 days
* **Fun Fact:** The high-stakes festival involved the ritual humiliation of the King, who had to strip away his royal crown and insignia before being slapped by the high priest to prove his humility to the god Marduk.

**487. What is the total number of primary Olympian gods who sat on the grand council seats of Mount Olympus in Greek myth?**

* **Answer:** 12
* **Fun Fact:** While Hades was one of the premier Greek gods, he did not possess a seat on Mount Olympus, choosing to reside permanently inside his underworld domain.

**488. How many thousands of lines of verse make up the epic ancient Mesopotamian text known as *The Epic of Gilgamesh*?**

* **Answer:** 3.0 thousand lines
* **Fun Fact:** Carved into twelve separate clay tablets in cuneiform script, it stands as one of the earliest surviving masterpieces of world literature.

**489. In what year of the ancient calendar did Roman Emperor Augustus ban the ancient mystical rites of Isis within Rome's boundaries?**

* **Answer:** 28 BC
* **Fun Fact:** Augustus viewed the soaring popularity of Egyptian cults as a threat to traditional Roman religious values, especially following his bitter war against Mark Antony and Cleopatra.

**490. How many distinct heads did the terrifying guardian hound Cerberus traditionally possess in Greek underworld lore?**

* **Answer:** 3
* **Fun Fact:** Some ancient poets like Hesiod claimed the beast had up to 50 heads, but classical artists streamlined the design to three heads to represent past, present, and future.

**491. What is the total length of the famous epic Indian poem *The Mahabharata*, measured in thousands of verses?**

* **Answer:** 100 thousand verses
* **Fun Fact:** Containing over 1.8 million individual words, it is roughly ten times longer than the *Iliad* and *Odyssey* combined, making it the longest epic poem ever compiled.

**492. How many distinct animal shapes are built into the traditional calendar cycle of the Chinese Zodiac?**

* **Answer:** 12
* **Fun Fact:** According to ancient folk myth, the order of the animals was decided by a grand race hosted by the Jade Emperor, where the clever rat clinched first place by riding on the back of the ox.

**493. In what year of the ancient era did the Temple of Artemis at Ephesus—one of the Seven Wonders—get completely burned down by an arsonist seeking fame?**

* **Answer:** 356 BC
* **Fun Fact:** A man named Herostratus burned down the temple simply so his name would be written down in history, prompting citizens to pass a law banning anyone from ever speaking his name.

**494. How many distinct arms project from the cosmic dancing form of the Hindu god Shiva (Nataranja)?**

* **Answer:** 4
* **Fun Fact:** The arms are positioned to show cosmic balance: one hand holds the drum of creation, one holds the fire of destruction, one signals protection, and one points down to point toward liberation.

**495. What is the official height of the ancient monolithic stone circle pillars at Stonehenge, measured in feet?**

* **Answer:** 13 feet
* **Fun Fact:** The towering sarsen stones weigh roughly 25 tons each, topped by heavy stone lintels locked together using precise mortise and tenon wood-style joints.

**496. How many thousands of individual verses make up the grand Norse poetic text known as the *Poetic Edda*?**

* **Answer:** 7.0 thousand
* **Fun Fact:** Compiled in the 13th century from older oral folklore, it serves as the primary historical source for our understanding of Norse mythology and heroic legends.

**497. In what year did Roman construction workers finish building the massive Temple of Jupiter in Baalbek, featuring the largest stone blocks ever cut?**

* **Answer:** 60 AD
* **Fun Fact:** The foundation platform contains three massive stones known as the "Trilithon," each weighing an astronomical 800 tons, perfectly fitted together without mortar.

**498. How many separate daughters make up the council of the Muses, the Greek goddesses of science, literature, and the arts?**

* **Answer:** 9
* **Fun Fact:** Born to Zeus and Mnemosyne (the goddess of memory), the nine sisters include Calliope (epic poetry), Clio (history), and Melpomene (tragedy).

**499. What is the total width of the massive ancient earthwork labyrinth known as the Glastonbury Tor maze, in feet?**

* **Answer:** 500 feet
* **Fun Fact:** The ancient terraced maze wraps around a steep hill in England, associated in Arthurian lore with the mystical island of Avalon.

**500. How many millions of individual prayers are said to be carved into the stone walls of the ancient rock-cut temples of Ellora in India?**

* **Answer:** 2 million
* **Fun Fact:** Carved entirely out of a single basalt cliffside from the top down, the complex features the Kailasa temple, the largest monolithic rock excavation on Earth.

**501. In what year did archaeologists discover the intact library of King Ashurbanipal in Nineveh, containing the flood tablet of Gilgamesh?**

# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

Draw your assigned words well enough that the guesser can match each drawing to the right pair. Sketchers earn points for clear drawings; the guesser earns points for correct matches. The player with the most points after all rounds wins.

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop)
- Paper and markers or pens for everyone (one sheet per sketcher per round)
- 3 or more players

## Setup

1. Enter every player's name into the app.
2. Choose the number of rounds. Each player will take one turn as the guesser per round.
3. Press **Start** to begin.

## Roles Each Round

**Guesser** — One player per round. Does not draw. Their job is to look at all the finished drawings and figure out which drawing belongs to which word pair.

**Sketchers** — Everyone else. Each sketcher secretly receives a unique word pair to draw (e.g., "dancing bear" or "angry cyclist").

## How to Play

### Step 1 — Sketchers Receive Their Words

Pass the device around the table. Each sketcher taps the screen to secretly view their assigned **descriptor + subject** pair (e.g., "melting snowman"). Do **not** show your word to anyone — especially not the guesser!

### Step 2 — Everyone Draws

All sketchers draw their word pair on paper at the same time. You may not write any letters, numbers, or symbols — only drawings!

Tip: Draw as clearly as you can. If the guesser can't figure out your drawing, you won't score any points.

### Step 3 — Guesser Makes Matches

Once everyone is done drawing, all sketches are laid out in view. The app shows the guesser all the descriptors and subjects that were assigned, plus some **decoy words** mixed in to make it harder.

The guesser tries to match each drawing to the correct descriptor + subject pair.

## Scoring

After the guesser announces their matches, score each sketcher individually:

| Guesser's Match | Sketcher earns | Guesser earns |
|---|---|---|
| Neither word correct | **0 pts** | **0 pts** |
| One word correct | **1 pt** | **0 pts** |
| Both words correct | **3 pts** | **2 pts** |

## End of Round

After scoring, the guesser role passes to the next player. Start a new round.

## Winning

After all rounds are complete, total up everyone's points. The player with the **most points** wins!

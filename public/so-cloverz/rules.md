# So Cloverz Rules

## Goal

Work together to reconstruct the original arrangement of clover cards using the clue-giver's one-word hints.

## Setup

1. Choose **1 or 2 additional tiles** — this determines how many cards are dealt (5 or 6 total).
2. Tap **Start Game**.

## The Clover Cards

Each card has **4 category words** — one on each edge (top, right, bottom, left). Adjacent edges between two touching cards share a connection that the clue-giver must hint at.

## Giving Clues (before Shuffle)

The clue-giver looks at all the dealt cards and **writes one word** on paper for each pair of words that will touch when the cards are arranged correctly.

- In a 2×2 grid there are **4 inner edges** (the places where two cards touch), so 4 clues total.
- Each clue connects exactly **2 words** — the word on one card's edge and the word on the adjacent card's edge touching it.
- Keep your clues secret until after the shuffle.

## Shuffling

Tap **Shuffle**. Four cards are randomly placed in a 2×2 grid, each rotated randomly. The **outer edge words** (highlighted in gold) are revealed to everyone — these are the words that face outward and don't need a clue.

One or two unused cards are shown below the grid as **Not Chosen**.

## Guessing

The clue-giver reads their clues aloud. The team discusses and tries to rearrange the cards (in their heads or on paper) to match the clues. The team earns a point for each correctly placed card.

## Shuffle Again / New Game

- **Shuffle** — reshuffle the same cards into a new random arrangement.
- **New Game** — return to setup and deal a fresh set of cards.

## Tips

- A good clue connects **two specific words** without accidentally fitting other nearby words.
- The trickier the connections, the more impressive — but make sure your team can figure them out!
- Unused cards are shown after the shuffle — use them to rule out possibilities.

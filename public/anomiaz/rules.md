# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

Earn the most points by winning face-offs against other players. Each turn a card is dealt to a player — when two players' cards share a matching colored symbol, they face off. The group judges who gave the worse answer, and the winner scores a point.

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop)
- 3 or more players

## Setup

1. Enter every player's name — one name per line.
2. Choose how many cards to include in the deck (100–500). More cards means a longer game.
3. Press **Start Game** to begin.

## How to Play

Players take turns in clockwise order. On your turn:

1. Press **Deal** to receive a card from the deck.
2. Your card shows a **category** (e.g., "A type of dog breed") and a **colored symbol** (e.g., a red triangle).
3. The card is placed face-up in front of you as your **top card**.
4. After each deal, check whether a face-off is triggered (see below).

## Face-Offs

A face-off is triggered when two players' top cards share a matching symbol. There are two ways this happens:

**Direct Match** — Two players have the exact same colored symbol on their top card.

**Wild Match** — A Wild card shows two different colored symbols. If one player's top card matches one symbol and another player's top card matches the other symbol, those two players face off.

### Resolving a Face-Off

1. Each player in the face-off gives an answer for **their own card's category**.
2. The group discusses and votes on who gave the **worse** answer.
3. Tap the losing player's card to remove it. The winning player earns **+1 point**.
4. With the losing card removed, the next card in their stack becomes their new top card. Check immediately for new matches — face-offs can chain together!

## Wild Cards

- When a Wild card is dealt, it is placed in the **center of the table**, not in front of any player.
- It displays two different colored symbols.
- It links any two players whose top cards show those two symbols, triggering a face-off between them.
- The Wild card stays in the center until its face-off is fully resolved.

## Scoring

- Win a face-off → **+1 point**
- Lose a face-off → no points; your top card is removed

## Winning

The game ends when all cards have been dealt and every face-off has been resolved. The player with the **most points** wins!

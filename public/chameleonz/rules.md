# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

If you are a regular player, find and expose the Chameleon hiding among you. If you are the Chameleon, blend in with convincing clues — and if caught, guess the secret word to still win.

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop)
- 4 or more players

## Setup

1. Gather everyone around the device.
2. The app will display a **category** and a **4×4 grid of 16 related words**.
3. One word in the grid is secretly highlighted as the **secret word** — only visible to regular players.
4. Pass the device around so each player can secretly check whether they are the Chameleon. The Chameleon sees the grid but does NOT see which word is highlighted.

## Roles

**Regular Players** — Know the secret word. Must give a clue convincing enough to show they know it, without making it so obvious the Chameleon can guess it.

**The Chameleon** — Does not know the secret word. Must give a believable clue anyway, to avoid being caught. There is exactly one Chameleon per round.

## How to Play

### Step 1 — Give Clues

Starting from any player and going clockwise, each player says **one word** as a clue related to the secret word.

- Regular players: give a clue that proves you know the word without giving it away too easily.
- The Chameleon: listen to the other clues to figure out the secret word, then say something that sounds like you know it.

### Step 2 — Discuss

After all clues have been given, players openly discuss who they think the Chameleon is. Any player can share their reasoning.

### Step 3 — Vote

Count down **3, 2, 1** — everyone simultaneously points to the player they suspect is the Chameleon. The player with the most fingers pointing at them is accused.

- If there is no clear majority (a tie), the Chameleon escapes and **wins**.

### Step 4 — The Chameleon's Last Chance

If the accused player is the Chameleon, they are caught — but they get **one final chance**: guess the secret word.

- If they guess correctly, the **Chameleon wins**.
- If they guess wrong, the **regular players win**.

If the accused player is NOT the Chameleon, the Chameleon escapes and **wins**.

## Winning

| Outcome | Winner |
|---|---|
| Chameleon not caught (no majority vote) | Chameleon |
| Chameleon caught but guesses the secret word | Chameleon |
| Chameleon caught and guesses wrong | Regular Players |
| Wrong person accused | Chameleon |

## Tips

- Regular players: clues that are too obvious help the Chameleon blend in. Be specific enough to prove your knowledge, but not so specific you hand them the answer.
- The Chameleon: pay close attention to the first few clues — they often reveal the secret word.

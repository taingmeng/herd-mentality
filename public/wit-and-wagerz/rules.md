# Wits & Wagers Rules

**Players:** 4–20 | **Time:** 25 minutes

## Goal
After 7 questions, the player or team with the most points wins.

## Setup
- Each player gets 1 Answer Card, 1 dry-erase pen, and 2 Betting Chips.
- Choose a Banker and a Question Reader.
- The Question Reader draws 7 Question Cards and places them face-down.

## Each Round

### 1. Answer
The Question Reader reads the question. Everyone has **30 seconds** to write a number guess on their Answer Card. Try to be as close as possible without going over.

### 2. Sort
Place all Answer Cards face-up on the Betting Mat, arranged from smallest to largest guess.
- Duplicate guesses go in the same slot.
- With an **odd** number of unique guesses, the middle guess goes in the "Pays 2 to 1" slot.
- With an **even** number of unique guesses, the two middle guesses go in the "Pays 3 to 1" slots and the "Pays 2 to 1" slot is left empty.

### 3. Bet
Players have **30 seconds** to place their 2 Betting Chips on any slot that has a guess (or "All Guesses Too High"). You can split your chips or put both on one slot. You can move bets during the 30 seconds, but not after time is up.

From round 2 onward, you can also bet poker chips won from previous rounds (red = 1 pt, blue = 5 pts). No limit on how many chips you can bet, but still max 2 bets per round.

### 4. Reveal
The Question Reader reveals the correct answer. The **winning slot** is the guess closest to the correct answer without going over. If all guesses are too high, "All Guesses Too High" wins.

### 5. Score
- **Correct guess bonus:** +3 red chips to each player whose guess was in the winning slot.
- **Winning bets** are paid out at the slot's odds. Your Betting Chips are always returned (win or lose). Poker chips are lost if you bet wrong.

| Slot | Payout |
|------|--------|
| Pays 2 to 1 | Win 2× your bet |
| Pays 3 to 1 | Win 3× your bet |
| Pays 4 to 1 | Win 4× your bet |
| Pays 5 to 1 | Win 5× your bet |
| Pays 6 to 1 | Win 6× your bet |

## Winning
The player or team with the most points after 7 questions wins. Ties go to the team with the youngest player.

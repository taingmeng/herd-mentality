# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

If you are an artist, expose the Fake Artist hiding among you. If you are the Fake Artist, blend in without knowing the subject — and if caught, guess the word to still win.

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop)
- One large sheet of paper shared by everyone
- A different colored pen, marker, or crayon for each player
- 4 or more players

## Setup

1. Give each player a pen or marker in a **different color**. Colors matter — this is how you know who drew each stroke.
2. Place one large sheet of paper in the center of the table where everyone can reach.
3. Open the app. The app will reveal a **category** and a **secret word** to all players — except the Fake Artist, who only sees the category.
4. Pass the device around so each player can secretly view their role and the word (or lack of it). Do not react or reveal anything!

## Roles

**Artists** — Know the secret word. Draw strokes that hint at the word without making it too obvious, so the Fake Artist can't figure it out.

**Fake Artist** — Knows only the category, not the secret word. Must draw something believable based on what others draw, hoping no one notices.

There is exactly **one Fake Artist** per round.

## How to Play

### Step 1 — Draw One Stroke Each (×2 Rounds)

Starting from any player and going clockwise, each player adds **exactly one continuous stroke** to the shared drawing using their colored pen.

Everyone draws **2 rounds** (2 strokes each total). You may not lift your pen mid-stroke, and you may not write any letters or numbers.

**Tip for artists:** Draw something that shows you know the word, but don't make it so obvious that the Fake Artist immediately figures it out.

**Tip for the Fake Artist:** Watch what others draw carefully. Try to add a stroke that fits in naturally.

### Step 2 — Discuss

After all strokes are drawn, players look at the finished drawing and openly discuss who they think the Fake Artist is. You can point out suspicious strokes or defend your own.

### Step 3 — Vote

Count down **3, 2, 1** — everyone simultaneously points to the player they believe is the Fake Artist.

- If no single player receives the most votes (a tie), the Fake Artist **wins**.
- If a player is clearly accused (most votes), they reveal their identity.

### Step 4 — Fake Artist's Last Chance

If the accused player is the Fake Artist, they are caught — but they get **one final chance**: guess the secret word.

- If they guess correctly, the **Fake Artist wins**.
- If they guess wrong, the **Artists win**.

If the accused player is NOT the Fake Artist, the Fake Artist escapes and **wins**.

## Winning

| Outcome | Winner |
|---|---|
| Fake Artist not caught (tied vote) | Fake Artist |
| Fake Artist caught but guesses the word correctly | Fake Artist |
| Fake Artist caught and guesses wrong | Artists |
| Wrong person accused | Fake Artist |

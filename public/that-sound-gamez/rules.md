# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

Get your teammates to guess as many words as possible using only **sounds and body movements** — no hands allowed! The first team to reach the target score wins.

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop) — one device shared between both teams
- 4 or more players, split into 2 teams

## Setup

1. Split all players into **two teams**.
2. Choose a **game mode:**
   - **Random Category** — Each round is limited to one specific category of words.
   - **All Categories** — Words come from any category, mixed randomly.
3. Set a **target score**. The first team to reach this score wins the game.

## How to Play

Teams take turns. On each turn:

1. One player from the active team holds the device so they can see the word on screen — but their teammates cannot see it.
2. They have **60 seconds** to get their team to guess as many words as possible.

**The key rule: your hands must be behind your back.** Use only:
- Sounds (animal noises, sound effects, humming, etc.)
- Body movements (posture, facial expressions, head movements)

No words, no mouthing words, no hand gestures.

### During Your Turn

- When your team guesses a word correctly, press **Correct (+1)** to score the point and move to the next word.
- If a word is too difficult, press **Skip (−1)** to skip it — but this costs your team 1 point, so use it wisely.
- Try to get through as many words as possible before time runs out!

## End of a Turn

After 60 seconds the timer stops. A summary of the round is shown — words guessed and your score for that turn. The other team then takes their turn.

## Winning

The first team to reach the **target score** wins the game!

## Tips

- Think about iconic sounds or movements associated with the word — what does a penguin walk like? What sound does a train make?
- Your teammates can shout out guesses freely — the more they call out, the better.
- Skipping costs a point, but getting stuck on one word wastes precious time. Know when to move on.

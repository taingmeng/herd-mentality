# OVERVIEW OF DICE POOL PARTYZ

Roll 12 colorful dice and score them across 12 unique categories. Every roll only counts once — choose wisely to maximize your total!

Dice Pool Partyz has two modes:

* **Dice Roll Only** — Roll and reveal the results for everyone to see. Great as a shared activity or party icebreaker.
* **Roll and Write** — The full game. Play through 12 rounds, assigning each roll to one scoring category. The game ends when all 12 categories have been filled. Highest score wins!

# SETUP

1. Open the game on a phone or tablet where everyone can see.
2. Choose your mode: **Dice Roll Only** or **Roll and Write**.
3. In Roll and Write, each player uses their own device — or pass one device around and take turns.

# THE DICE

Each roll throws 12 dice across 6 colors — 2 dice per color:

* Red
* Blue
* Green
* Pink
* Purple
* Orange

# GAMEPLAY (ROLL AND WRITE)

**Round structure:**

1. Tap **Roll!** (or press Space) to throw all 12 dice. The dice animate in the tray for a few seconds.
2. The tray hides after the countdown. Before revealing, **pick a scoring category** from the scorecard.
3. Tap **Reveal Results** to uncover the dice and score your chosen category.
4. Tap **Roll Again** to advance to the next round.

Repeat for all 12 rounds. Once a category is used, it cannot be chosen again.

# SCORING CATEGORIES

| Category | How to Score |
|---|---|
| Red Dice | Sum of both red dice |
| Blue Dice | Sum of both blue dice |
| Green Dice | Sum of both green dice |
| Pink Dice | Sum of both pink dice |
| Purple Dice | Sum of both purple dice |
| Orange Dice | Sum of both orange dice |
| Perfect Pairs | Any pair of same-color dice showing the same value = 10 pts each (up to 60 pts) |
| Low (1–2–3) | Sum of all dice showing 1, 2, or 3 |
| Fours | Sum of all dice showing 4 |
| Fives | Sum of all dice showing 5 |
| Sixes | Sum of all dice showing 6 |
| Pool Partyz! | Sum of all 12 dice |

# STRATEGY TIPS

* **Perfect Pairs** can be huge — save it for a roll where you've got matching pairs across multiple colors.
* **Pool Partyz!** scores the total of all 12 dice, so it's best used on a high-rolling round.
* **Low (1–2–3)** is a good safety valve for a roll that didn't cooperate.
* Color categories (Red, Blue, etc.) each cap at 12 — useful for dumping a low roll you can't use elsewhere.

# END OF GAME

After 12 rounds, your final score is displayed with a full breakdown by category. Compare with other players to see who scored highest!

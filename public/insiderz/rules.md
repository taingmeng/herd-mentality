# {{ GAME_NAME }}

## Objective

A secret word must be guessed through yes/no questions — but hidden among the questioners is an **Insider** who already knows the word and is secretly steering the group toward it. After the word is found, everyone must identify who the Insider was. The Insider wins by staying hidden (or by correctly guessing the secret word if caught).

## What You Need

- This app (phone, tablet, or laptop)
- 4 or more players (works best with 5–8)

## Roles

There are three roles, assigned randomly by the app each round:

**Master (1 player)** — Knows the secret word. Answers only "Yes," "No," or "I don't know" to questions. Declares themselves publicly at the start.

**Insider (1 player)** — Also knows the secret word. Pretends to be a Common player. Secretly tries to guide the group toward the correct answer through clever questions — without being too obvious.

**Commons (everyone else)** — Don't know the secret word. Ask questions to figure it out. Also try to spot who the Insider is.

## Setup

1. Enter all player names into the app.
2. Pass the device around so each player can **secretly** check their assigned role. Do not reveal your role — except the Master.
3. The **Master announces themselves** to the group. The app also shows the Master's name after role assignment.
4. The Insider stays silent about their role and pretends to be a Common player.

## Phase 1 — Ask Questions (5 Minutes)

Commons and the Insider take turns asking the Master **yes/no questions** to figure out the secret word. There is no fixed order — anyone can ask at any time.

**Master can only answer:**
- "Yes"
- "No"
- "I don't know"

The Insider's goal is to secretly steer the group toward the correct word by asking leading questions — but without making it obvious they already know the answer.

This phase ends the moment a player asks "Is it [word]?" and the Master says "Yes." Remember who asked that winning question — this is the **guesser** for the voting phase.

**If no one guesses the word in 5 minutes, everyone loses.**

## Phase 2 — Discuss (1 Minute)

Once the secret word is found, start a 1-minute discussion timer using the app.

Everyone — including the Master — openly discusses who they think the Insider is. Review the questions that were asked. Who asked suspiciously leading questions? Who seemed to know too much?

You may end the discussion a little early or extend it slightly, but keep it focused.

## Phase 3 — Vote: Was the Guesser the Insider?

The guesser (who asked the final correct question) does **not vote** in this round. Everyone else votes simultaneously.

**How to vote:** Put your fist on the table. On the count of 3, show **thumbs up** if you think the guesser is the Insider. Keep your fist closed if you think they are not.

- **Majority says yes → but the guesser is a Common:** The vote was wrong. Proceed to the second vote.
- **Majority says yes → and the guesser is the Insider:** Insider is caught! But see Phase 4.
- **No clear majority:** The Insider wins.

## Phase 4 — Second Vote: Who Is the Insider?

(Only reached if the guesser was a Common and most players suspected them.)

All players — including the guesser — simultaneously point to who they believe is the Insider.

- If there is a **tie**, the guesser's vote counts as **2 votes**.
- **Majority points to the Insider → Commons and Master win.**
- **No majority or wrong person chosen → Insider wins.**

## Winning

| Outcome | Winner |
|---|---|
| No one guesses the secret word in time | Everyone loses |
| Insider is correctly identified by vote | Master & Commons |
| Insider is not caught | Insider |
| Wrong person is accused | Insider |
